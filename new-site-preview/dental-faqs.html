Pain & emergencies

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, swelling of the gum or face, a broken or knocked-out tooth, and bleeding that will not stop after an extraction. Call the clinic before you travel so the dentist can fit you in. Dental emergency →

When should I go to a hospital instead of a dentist?

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if swelling spreads towards the eye or neck, or if you have fever, difficulty breathing or difficulty swallowing. Dental emergency →

What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?

Hold it by the crown, not the root. Rinse briefly in milk, put it back in the socket if you can or keep it in milk, and get to a dentist immediately. The first hour matters most. Dental emergency →

How much does a root canal cost at Goel Dental?

A root canal costs ₹4,500 for a front tooth, ₹5,500 for a premolar and ₹6,500 for a molar. Single-sitting is ₹7,500 and microscopic ₹8,500. Back teeth usually also need a crown, from ₹6,500. Your exact fee is given in writing after the exam. Root canal treatment →

Does a root canal hurt?

The tooth is numbed with local anaesthetic first, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. Mild soreness for a few days afterwards is common and settles with ordinary pain relief. Laughing-gas sedation is available for anxious patients. Root canal treatment →

How many visits does a root canal take?

Most root canals take one to three visits of 15–30 minutes. Some teeth, with little infection and no swelling, can be finished in one 30–60 minute sitting. The endodontist decides after the X-ray. Root canal treatment →

Root canals

How long does a single-sitting root canal take?

Usually 30–60 minutes from start to finish, in one appointment. Single-sitting root canal →

Can every tooth have a single-sitting root canal?

No. It suits teeth with little or no infection. Teeth with pus, swelling, severe pain or a failed earlier root canal usually need more than one visit so the infection can settle first. Single-sitting root canal →

How much does a single-sitting root canal cost?

₹7,500 on the clinic's 2026 fee list, plus a crown if the tooth needs one. Single-sitting root canal →

What is a microscopic root canal?

A root canal done under a dental microscope with up to 25× magnification. It helps find very fine extra canals, see cracks and retrieve broken instruments, so it is used for molars, re-treatments and difficult teeth. Microscopic root canal →

When do I need a microscopic root canal?

When a molar has hard-to-find canals, a previous root canal has failed, a tooth may be cracked, or an instrument has broken inside a canal. Most routine root canals do not need the microscope. Microscopic root canal →

How much does a microscopic root canal cost?

₹8,500 on the clinic's 2026 fee list, compared with ₹4,500–6,500 for a standard root canal. Microscopic root canal →

Implants & crowns

How much does a dental implant cost at Goel Dental?

From ₹17,000 per implant (Adin or Alpha Bio) to ₹55,000 (Straumann), with Osstem at ₹35,000 and Nobel Biocare at ₹40,000–45,000. The crown on top is extra, ₹8,500–17,000. The consultation is free. Dental implants →

Is the implant consultation free?

Yes, according to the clinic's 2026 fee list. A CBCT 3D scan, if needed for planning, costs ₹2,500–4,500. Dental implants →

How long does the implant process take?

Usually a few months: the implant is placed, heals into the bone, and then the crown is fitted. It takes longer if bone grafting or a sinus lift is needed first. Dental implants →

What is All-on-4?

A fixed set of teeth for a whole jaw held by four implants. The back implants are angled to use the bone that is available, which often avoids extensive bone grafting. All-on-4 / All-on-6 implants →

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?

All-on-6 uses six implants instead of four, spreading the biting load further for extra stability. It can help when bone density is lower. All-on-4 / All-on-6 implants →

How much does All-on-4 cost?

₹90,000–1,00,000 per arch on the clinic's 2026 fee list. All-on-4 / All-on-6 implants →

How much does a dental crown cost?

A metal-ceramic crown is ₹6,500, a zirconia crown ₹8,000 and a same-day CAD/CAM crown ₹12,000–14,000 on the clinic's 2026 fee list. Crowns & bridges →

Can I get a crown in one day?

Often yes. The tooth is scanned, and the crown is designed and milled on site with a Dentsply Sirona CAD/CAM system, then fitted in the same appointment. Crowns & bridges →

Which crown is best after a root canal?

Metal-free zirconia or ceramic crowns look most natural and are strong; metal-ceramic crowns are a lower-cost choice for back teeth. Ask to see samples before deciding. Crowns & bridges →

Extractions

Does every wisdom tooth need to be removed?

No. If a wisdom tooth causes no pain and shows no problem on the X-ray, it can stay. Removal is advised for repeated pain or swelling, deep decay, damage to the next tooth, or a cyst. Wisdom tooth extraction →

How much does wisdom tooth removal cost?

From ₹2,500 per tooth. The fee depends on how the tooth is positioned, which the on-site OPG X-ray shows. Wisdom tooth extraction →

How long is recovery after wisdom tooth removal?

Most people return to normal routine the next day. Swelling can last a few days, especially after lower wisdom teeth; cold compresses straight after the extraction help. Wisdom tooth extraction →

How much does a tooth extraction cost?

A simple extraction is ₹500–2,500 per tooth on the clinic's published price list. Wisdom teeth start from ₹2,500. Tooth extraction →

Does tooth extraction hurt?

The area is numbed with local anaesthetic, so you feel pressure but not sharp pain. Mild soreness afterwards usually settles in a few days. Tooth extraction →

What should I avoid after an extraction?

For 24 hours avoid rinsing, spitting, smoking, straws and hot drinks. Bite on gauze for 30 minutes and eat soft, cool food the first day. Tooth extraction →

Cleaning & gums

How much does teeth cleaning cost?

Scaling and polishing is ₹2,500 on the clinic's 2026 fee list. Teeth cleaning →

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Every six months suits most people. People with gum disease, braces or implants may need cleaning more often. Teeth cleaning →

Does scaling damage enamel?

No. Scaling removes hardened tartar, not enamel. Teeth can feel sensitive for a few days afterwards. Teeth cleaning →

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

Bleeding when brushing is the most common early sign of gum disease (gingivitis), caused by plaque and tartar at the gumline. A professional cleaning and better brushing and flossing usually reverse it. Gum treatment →

Is gum disease curable?

Early gum disease is reversible with cleaning and good home care. Advanced gum disease can be controlled with deep cleaning, laser treatment and regular maintenance, but lost bone does not grow back on its own. Gum treatment →

What does a periodontist do?

A periodontist is a gum specialist. At Goel Dental, Dr Ambica Sharma assesses gum pockets, plans deep cleaning and laser gum treatment, and manages maintenance. Gum treatment →

Braces & aligners

What types of braces are available?

Metal braces, tooth-coloured ceramic braces, lingual braces fixed behind the teeth, and removable clear aligners. Braces →

Can adults get braces?

Yes. Braces and aligners work for adults as well as teenagers, provided the gums and bone are healthy. Braces →

At what age should a child see an orthodontist?

An early check around age 7–8 can spot jaw and bite problems before all adult teeth arrive. Braces →

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

A typical case takes about a year, using 18–30 aligners changed about every two weeks, with check-ups about monthly. Clear aligners →

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

For most crowding and spacing, yes. Severe bite problems may still need fixed braces. The orthodontist decides after a scan. Clear aligners →

Can I eat with aligners in?

No. Take them out to eat and to drink anything other than water, then brush before putting them back. Clear aligners →

Children & fillings

When should my child first see a dentist?

When the first tooth appears, or by the first birthday at the latest. Children's dentistry →

Do milk teeth need fillings if they will fall out?

Yes. An infected milk tooth can harm the adult tooth forming beneath it, and losing milk teeth early can cause crowding later. Children's dentistry →

How often should children have fluoride application?

Every 4–6 months. It takes about two minutes and strengthens enamel against cavities. Children's dentistry →

How much does a tooth filling cost?

Tooth-coloured fillings start from ₹800. A microscope-assisted filling under rubber dam is ₹2,000, and ceramic inlays or onlays ₹10,000–14,000. Tooth fillings →

How long does a tooth-coloured filling last?

Many years with good care. Large fillings on back teeth wear faster and may later need an inlay or crown. Tooth fillings →

Can a cavity heal on its own?

No. Once decay has made a cavity it needs a filling. Left untreated it can reach the nerve and need a root canal. Tooth fillings →
