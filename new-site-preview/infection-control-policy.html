Patient safety

Infection Control at Goel Dental

Quick answer

Goel Dental prevents cross-infection between patients and staff with a set of measures: sterilised instruments, disinfected surfaces and rooms, single-use items where applicable, surface barriers, biomedical waste management and pest control.

Why it matters

Infections such as hepatitis B and C, HIV and tuberculosis can spread through unsafe practice in any clinic. Each measure below reduces that risk.

What we do

  • Instruments: cleaned and sterilised between patients Add autoclave type and pouch/indicator system
  • Surfaces: disinfected between patients
  • Rooms: whole-room disinfection
  • Single-use items where applicable
  • Barriers: touch surfaces covered
  • Biomedical waste: segregated and disposed of under regulations
  • Pest control service

The clinic’s NABH accreditation is listed on the home page. Confirm NABH certificate number and validity

Ask us

You can ask how any instrument used on you was sterilised. This right is in our patient charter.

Questions

Are dental instruments reused?

Metal instruments are sterilised between patients; items meant for single use are discarded after one patient.
