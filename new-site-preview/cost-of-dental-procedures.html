Fees

Dental Treatment Fees at Goel Dental

Quick answer

Goel Dental publishes its fees. Consultation is ₹500 and implant or cosmetic consultations are free; a root canal costs ₹4,500–6,500, implants ₹17,000–55,000 by brand, and crowns ₹6,500–14,000. Your exact fee is confirmed in writing after the exam.

Questions patients ask

How much is a dental consultation at Goel Dental?

A general consultation is ₹500. Implant and cosmetic consultations are free on the 2026 fee list.

Why can the final fee differ from the list?

The exam may show you need extra work, such as a crown after a root canal, or a different material. The dentist gives the fee in writing before starting.

Do you offer EMI?

Yes, on leading credit cards. Ask the front desk which cards and tenures apply.
