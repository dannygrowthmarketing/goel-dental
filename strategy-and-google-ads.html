Finding

Local beats "Delhi"

"dentist in malviya nagar" converted at 2–3% for ₹960–1,334. "dentist south delhi" cost ₹20,430 per conversion. The catchment is real and narrow.

Finding

People want prices

"dentist near me with fees", "teeth cleaning price delhi", "tooth cap price", "implant cost in delhi" are recurring searches with high CTR. Both new sites publish fees.

Finding

Premium never had a page

Implant searches clicked at 21–31% CTR but implant ads were disapproved and veneer traffic (₹37,783) produced zero conversions with no dedicated landing page.

Finding

Areas that searched

Saket, Chhattarpur, Munirka, Vasant Kunj, Kalkaji and Karol Bagh all appear in search terms. Karol Bagh is ~15 km away; its campaign had most ads disapproved.

Finding

Brand is cheap

"goel dental", "goel dental clinic malviya nagar": ₹548–2,060 per conversion. A small brand campaign protects it from competitors.

Finding

Quality Score drag

Near-me exact and root canal keywords show "Rarely shown (low Quality Score)" and below first-page bid. Better landing pages lower the price per click.

07

Before ads restart.

Restarting campaigns on today's tracking would repeat the last ten years. Fix first, then spend.

Week 1

Clean the account

Clear the overdue balance only when ready to relaunch. Remove page views as conversions; one primary conversion: qualified lead. Fix disapprovals and claims.

Week 1–2

Install measurement

GTM, GA4 key events, call tracking, WhatsApp ref codes, CRM. Test 20 enquiries end to end.

Week 3–4

Relaunch narrow

Local core + root canal + implants on the new pages, 5–8 km, during staffed hours. Cosmetic on DDS after.
